# ⭐ What is PreGPT 2.0 ?

PreGPT 2.0 is the world’s first high performing, scalable, privacy focused generative AI chatbot that strictly sources its compute power via a decentralized network from our proud partner _Salad.com or Venice.AI_.  PreGPT 2.0 utilizes purely open-sourced LLM models.  Users can access it through their Presearch account for a monthly fee, payable in PRE tokens.

* **The Basic Plan** ($2/month) powered by Salad.com’s decentralized network with MISTRAL7b model for standard AI chat capabilities, training data cutoff is roughly July 2023 and limited capabilities in languages other than English.
* **Pro Account:** Pro Plan ($5/month) running Venice.ai API with higher-powered uncensored models for advanced AI features, more recent training data and multi-language support.

## Safety and Privacy

PreGPT 2.0 leverages unique privacy framework, ensuring that:

* We do not store any chat content by default. If you choose to store chats, they are private and permanently deleted upon removal. No logs or prompts are stored on servers.
* Inference requests and responses are encrypted and streamed directly to and from decentralized GPUs.
* PreGPT is designed with privacy in mind and it’s worth noting that this practice sets us apart from most, as most other chatbots incorporate user chats to enhance their training. We however, are non-training by default.
