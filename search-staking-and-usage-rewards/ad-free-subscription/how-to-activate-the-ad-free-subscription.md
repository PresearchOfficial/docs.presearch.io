# How to activate the Ad-Free Subscription

1. **Select the hamburger icon.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (156).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. **Go to My Account (**[https://presearch.com/account](https://presearch.com/account)**).**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (157).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. **Select ad-free Subscription (**[https://account.presearch.com/tokens/ad-free-subscription](https://account.presearch.com/tokens/ad-free-subscription)**).**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (158).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4.  **Select either PRE or Stripe (Credit Card)**&#x20;

    **Then select Subscribe to Ad-Free Standard.**&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (159).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. &#x20;**If you selected PRE, Select Yes, subscribe.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (160).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. &#x20;**If you selected Stripe (Credit Card, Complete the Stripe form for payment with credit card or otherwise, selecting >Subscribe when done.**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (161).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. &#x20;**Wait for Stripe to confirm the transaction back to Presearch .**

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (162).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

hat's it, you can now have an improved search experience without ads.
