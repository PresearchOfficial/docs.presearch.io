# How do I export keyword stats?

&#x20;Go to your Keyword Dashboard, scroll to the bottom of the first page, and click the “Export&#x20;keywords to CSV” link below the keyword data table. The file will download automatically to your&#x20;computer. If the export isn’t working or you're unable to download the file, feel free to contact the&#x20;Presearch team directly, we can forward the stats to you manually.
