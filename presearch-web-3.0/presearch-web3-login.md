# Presearch Web3 Login

1. Go to [presearch.com](https://presearch.com/)
2. Click on the hamburger menu in the top right corner of the page.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (178).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on sing in Sing In | Create Account / Sign into Your Account.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (179).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Enter Log In.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/lolo.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Log in with Web3.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/conectar.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

6. Click on Web3 Log in

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/ver2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

7. Sign the external wallet login request by clicking the confirm button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/billetera1.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

8. Once you have connected your external wallet via Web3, you will be logged into your Presearch account.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/ingreso2.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/calidad.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
