# Doppelgänger

Presearch, the privacy-centric decentralized search engine, announced in February 2026 the launch of Doppelgänger, an innovative image-powered creator discovery tool integrated into its opt-in Spicy Mode (NSFW search experience).

Users can upload or drag-and-drop an image to instantly find visually similar adult creators on OnlyFans, with quick previews via a Skyscraper widget (3 results) and a full tab showing up to 40 matches, powered by Explore.Fans.The feature aims to improve ethical discoverability for creators in a restrictive NSFW landscape—bypassing algorithmic suppression and platform limitations—while prioritizing privacy (no tracking, profiling, or behavioral data collection), consent, and user intent. It contrasts with irresponsible AI image tools by focusing on real creators and revenue conversion. Spicy Mode remains age-gated, RTA-compliant, and toggle-activated, supporting a creator-first ecosystem with high-engagement privacy-first ads.

Presearch executives emphasized responsible innovation to benefit the adult creator economy without recklessness.
