# Presearch Launches NSFW Spicy Mode with OnlyFans Filtering Tool

&#x20;Presearch has launched NSFW Spicy Mode, a groundbreaking feature set designed to enhance discoverability and visibility for adult content creators, starting with an exclusive OnlyFans Creator Search. This first-of-its-kind discovery layer on a generalist search engine addresses the growing challenges faced by SFW and NSFW creators due to censorship, shadow-banning, and platform restrictions, such as Pornhub bans in 17 US states and parts of Europe. The new “Creators” tab offers smart filters—including price, attributes, likes, region, and free trial status—enabling users to explore OnlyFans profiles by username, display name, bio, and more, solving OnlyFans’ lack of native search functionality.

**Key Features:**&#x20;

1. Creator-First Discovery Tool: Includes an exclusive OnlyFans filtering integration, enabling users to search profiles by subscription price, region, likes, and free trial status—functionality not natively available on OnlyFans.



2. Privacy-Centric Advertising: Utilizes Presearch’s non-profiling Takeover Advertising (PTA) model, ensuring compliance with Restricted to Adults (RTA) standards while avoiding invasive tracking.



3. User Experience: Activated via a chili-pepper icon in the search bar with age verification (18+), Spicy Mode offers a curated “after-hours” experience with rotating background ads from adult brands like Lovense, Stripchat, and OnlyFinder.



4. Impact and Vision: Serving approximately 12 million monthly searches, Presearch aims to empower sex-positive creators by restoring visibility and providing a judgment-free space.&#x20;

CEO Tim Enneking emphasized the historical role of adult brands in internet development, positioning Spicy Mode as a response to big tech’s restrictive policies. The feature is freely accessible at [presearch.com](https://presearch.com/), aligning with Presearch’s mission to reward users with PRE tokens while fostering ethical advertising for underserved industries.
