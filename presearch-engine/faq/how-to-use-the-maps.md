# How to use the Maps

We’re excited to bring you Presearch Maps, powered by a new partnership with Apple! 🌍🔍 Enjoy private, fast, and user-first mapping—without compromising your data.

To activate maps, enter the location or country in the search bar followed by the words "map", "location", "address", "directions", "direction", "route". For example, "America Map", "London Location".

Search smarter, explore freely. Try Presearch Maps today!&#x20;

✅ Privacy-first navigation ✅ Accurate & reliable results ✅ Seamless Web3-friendly experience.

**Update:**&#x20;

Have you noticed Presearch maps are smoother now? Here are the patches we have made:

1. Map trigger words by themselves now activate maps - they don't require a second "place" word   \
   💎See list below of all map trigger keywords in the various supported languages   \
   💎Non-Latin characters are not supported
2. There is a map icon to turn on maps (without searching using a map trigger keyword)   \
   💎To turn off maps, simply do a search
3. From/To is now supported to bring up the map with the starting point and destination filled in (e.g., from Bolzano Italy to Venice)   \
   💎See list below of from/to words in each supported language
4. When you enter a starting point/destination to initiate driving directions you can simply hit enter
5. There is a new search function to show you a location for when maps are up, but you don't need directions
