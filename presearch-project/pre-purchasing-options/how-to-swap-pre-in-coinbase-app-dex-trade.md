# How to Swap PRE in Coinbase APP DEX Trade

1.  **Create a Coinbase account**

    \
    Download the Coinbase app and start the sign up process. You will need a valid ID and may be asked for proof of address in order to transact. Verifying your ID may take longer than a few minutes, depending on where you live.


2.  **Add a payment method**

    \
    Connect and fund your account using crypto or traditional payment methods, such as a debit card.



3.  &#x20;**Search for Presearch**

    \
    Tap the Home search bar to find Presearch. Its asset page will show a “DEX” tag above its chart to indicate exclusive availability via decentralized exchanges on the Ethereum, or Base networks.


4.  **Start a DEX trade**

    \
    Click “Buy & Sell” below to initiate a DEX trade. Click “Pay with” any amount of USDC (or other available USD funds) and carefully review the amount of Presearch you want to buy while noting its price details and fees.



5.  **Complete your DEX trade**

    \
    Once your DEX trade is completed on a decentralized exchange, your new Presearch balance can now be easily managed in your Coinbase portfolio.

    \
