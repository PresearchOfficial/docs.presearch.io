# How to buy PRE in AhoraCrypto.com

**Step 1**

Enter the platform [ahoracrypto.com](https://ahoracrypto.com/)



**Step 2**

**Select a cryptocurrency.**

&#x20;Choose the cryptocurrency you want to buy, which in this case will be PRE, and how much money you want to invest.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (139).png" alt="" width="298"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3**

Enter your wallet address. If you don't have a wallet, don't worry! Ahoracrypto will create a wallet for you for free and make sure you select the correct network; in this case, the ETH network.

**Step 4**

**Verify your identity**&#x20;

To prevent fraud and money laundering, we need to verify your identity. Ahoracrypto will ask you for an email, a phone number, a selfie and a photo of your ID.

**Step 5**

**Pay with your credit card**

You only have to enter your payment details, confirm the order and receive your PRE instantly.
