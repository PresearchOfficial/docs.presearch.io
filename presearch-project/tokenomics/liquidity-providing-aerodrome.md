# Liquidity Providing Aerodrome

## How to provide liquidity?

Liquidity can be provided in a few easy steps:

1. Visit [Aerodrome.Finance](https://aerodrome.finance/tokenlist?query=pre) and connect your wallet. Make sure you have some ETH for gas fees, the PRE and either USDC, AERO or WETH you wish to deposit in equal parts, and are connected on the Base Chain.

* _Add Custom Chain_…always use the Chain’s Main website docs for reference, do not simply search for the Chain ID. For Base, go [here.](https://docs.base.org/docs/using-base/)
* _Add Custom Token_ in your wallet — always make sure you are interacting with the official Base PRE contract found [here](https://basescan.org/address/0x3816dd4bd44c8830c2fa020a5605bac72fa3de7a). Correct token contracts can always be found on Coingecko.com or coinmarketcap.com.

2\. Select the [Liquidity](https://aerodrome.finance/tokenlist?query=pre) at the top:

3\. Choose First Token and Second Token i.e. PRE, then AERO

4\. Select the PRE pool you wish to contribute to and click “New Deposit”. Note whether it is a “Concentrated” Volatile Pool or “Basic” Volatile pool. Read more regarding the dynamics of each following the conclusion.

5\. Select token amount of the first assets listed and second asset will automatically populate in equal parts. Note, for Concentrated Volatile Pools, the spread listed by default is likely entirely too tight so please DYOR when adjusting strikes. For Basic Pools, you cannot manually adjust the strikes.

6\. Click “Allow PRE” and “Allow AERO” in Aerodrome and your wallet when prompted, then _Deposit_, then _Stake (100%)_ and you’re all set. Without staking you will not receive a portion of AERO emissions. Remember to be patient during this process and wait for Aerodrome, your wallet to sync and finish transactions before clicking on. Depending on the blockchain throughput, it can take longer. Check you dashboard to see your liquidity provided.
